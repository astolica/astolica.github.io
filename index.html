01 The Koi's Path

Legend says a koi that swims up the waterfall at the Dragon Gate becomes a dragon.

My name is Alec Teran, a student at the University of Arizona working toward a career in red teaming and offensive security. I'm drawn to understanding systems from the ground up, how memory, processes, and the kernel really work, so I can find where they may break.

Right now I'm building my foundation in C, Linux, and systems exploitation. Like the koi, I'm in it for the long climb.

04 Projects

Purple Team · AI Security

R3D

A fully local, autonomous purple team agent. It runs OSINT and active recon, correlates CVEs against a local NVD database, tests AI attack surfaces, and maps each finding to MITRE ATT&CK and to frameworks like NIST 800-53 and ISO 27001. A built-in verifier catches LLM hallucinations before they reach the report.

  • Python
  • Ollama
  • Nmap
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • GRC

Deception · Threat Detection

SweetSting

A honeypot and canary-credential framework for low-resource Linux systems. It serves a fake router login page, logs every attempt to MySQL, fires alerts when planted canary credentials are used, and shows activity live in a terminal dashboard. It's exposed through a Cloudflare Tunnel.
