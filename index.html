Projects

A mix of defensive tooling, infrastructure, and research. Most of it lives on my GitHub.

Honeypot & Deception

SweetSting

A Python honeypot and deception framework running on a Raspberry Pi 4. It serves a convincing fake Flask portal backed by MySQL with Argon2-hashed credentials, plants canary tripwires, fires email and webhook alerts, logs attacker behavior, and reports through a live Rich terminal dashboard. Isolated on its own VLAN with a Wazuh agent feeding my SIEM.

PythonFlaskMySQLArgon2WazuhRaspberry Pi
Log Analysis

sugarSearch.py

A companion log parser for SweetSting that chews through honeypot logs to flag brute-force source IPs and automated user agents. Built with the standard library: json, argparse, and collections.Counter, so it stays fast and dependency-free.

PythonargparseCounterlog parsing
AI Security Research

R3D-SIFT

An autonomous purple-team agent that runs fully local, no cloud APIs. One engagement produces red-team findings, blue-team telemetry for SIEM ingestion, and GRC compliance output at once: offensive recon, AI-surface testing, CVE correlation, and automatic mapping to frameworks like NIST 800-53, ISO 27001, and CIS Controls v8. The product of over a year of original work.

A Proxmox virtualization lab on a ThinkCentre M710q running a Wazuh SIEM manager, with VLAN isolation worked out across a GL.iNet router and managed switch. Designed toward a four-zone architecture, attack, target, blue team, and management, with pfSense as the internal lab firewall.
